CalPal's AI-powered food entry feature allows you to describe what you ate in plain English, and the AI will automatically extract nutritional information and create a food log for you.

## How It Works

The AI feature uses **Cloudflare Workers AI** to process natural language descriptions and estimate nutritional content based on common food databases and portion sizes.

<Info>
The AI analyzes your description to identify foods, estimate portions, and calculate nutritional values including calories, protein, carbs, fat, and even estimated cost.
</Info>

## Using AI Food Entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Input">
    Tap the floating + button on the home screen and select **"Tell the AI"** from the menu.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Describe Your Meal">
    Type a natural language description of what you ate. Be as specific or casual as you like.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap **"Create Log"** to send your description to the AI. The app will show a loading indicator while processing.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review">
    The AI will create a food log with estimated nutritional values. The entry will appear in your log history immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Value Normalization

The AI includes smart validation to ensure realistic values:

- All numeric values are clamped to reasonable ranges
- The AI can parse values like "780 kcal" or "45 g" automatically
- Invalid or missing values default to 0
- Total calories are limited to 3000 kcal per entry

<Note>
Value ranges are designed to catch unrealistic AI outputs while allowing for large meals and bulk food entries.
</Note>

## Restaurant Context

When combined with the restaurant menu feature, the AI can use specific menu data:

```javascript
// The AI receives restaurant menu context
{
  "description": "Big Mac with large fries",
  "restaurantId": "mcdonalds",
  "menu": [...] // Full McDonald's menu with exact nutritional data
}
```

This provides **more accurate estimates** when you mention specific restaurant items.

<Tip>
Mention the restaurant name in your description ("from McDonald's", "at Chick-fil-A") to help the AI match against known menu items.
</Tip>

## Limitations

<Warning>
AI estimates are **approximations** based on typical portions and standard recipes. For precise tracking, use barcode scanning or manual entry with exact measurements.
</Warning>

The AI may be less accurate for:
- Homemade meals with custom recipes
- Mixed dishes with many ingredients
- Unusual or regional foods not in training data
- Very large or very small portions

## Privacy & Data

<Info>
Your food descriptions are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI for processing. No data is stored on external servers - the AI returns nutritional estimates which are saved locally on your device.
</Info>

## Tips for Best Results

<Tip>
**Be Specific About Quantities** - Instead of "some chicken", try "6oz grilled chicken breast" for better estimates.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Mention Preparation Methods** - "fried chicken" vs "grilled chicken" will return different nutritional values.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Include Sides and Drinks** - The AI can handle multiple items: "burger, fries, and a soda"
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Use Restaurant Names** - Mentioning specific restaurants enables menu-specific accuracy.
</Tip>

## Error Handling

If the AI fails to process your input:

1. You'll see an error message: "Could not interpret your description"
2. Check your internet connection
3. Try rephrasing your description more simply
4. As a fallback, use manual food entry instead

## Technical Details

For developers and curious users:

- **Model**: Cloudflare Workers AI (specific model configurable in Worker)
- **Endpoint**: `https://calpalai.rishabh-1keshri.workers.dev/food-from-text`
- **Request Format**: JSON with `description` and optional `restaurantId` and `menu`
- **Response**: JSON with all nutritional fields
- **Timeout**: Standard HTTP request timeout

See the [useAI.ts](https://github.com/yourrepo/calpal/blob/main/hooks/useAI.ts) hook for implementation details.
