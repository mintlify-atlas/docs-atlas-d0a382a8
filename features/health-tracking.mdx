## Initial Profile Setup

When you first open CalPal, you'll be prompted to create your profile:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Unit System">
    Select between **US Standard** (ft/in, lbs) or **Metric** (cm, kg) units.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Age">
    Input your current age.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Height">
    - **US System**: Enter feet and inches in separate fields
    - **Metric System**: Enter height in centimeters
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Weight">
    - **US System**: Enter weight in pounds
    - **Metric System**: Enter weight in kilograms
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review BMI">
    Your BMI is calculated automatically and displayed with a color-coded indicator.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save Profile">
    Tap **Save** to create your profile and start tracking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Your profile is required to use CalPal. This ensures accurate health tracking and enables personalized insights.
</Info>

## Unit System Options

Choose the measurement system you're most comfortable with:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="US Standard">
    - Height: Feet and inches
    - Weight: Pounds (lbs)
    - BMI: Calculated using standard formula
    
    Example: 5 ft 10 in, 180 lbs → BMI 25.8
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Metric">
    - Height: Centimeters
    - Weight: Kilograms
    - BMI: Calculated using standard formula
    
    Example: 178 cm, 82 kg → BMI 25.9
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
Both systems use the same BMI calculation formula: weight (kg) / (height (m))². Conversions are handled automatically.
</Note>

## BMI Calculation & Color Coding

Your BMI (Body Mass Index) is automatically calculated and color-coded:

| BMI Range | Category | Color | Indicator |
|-----------|----------|-------|-----------|
| < 18.5 | Underweight | Blue 🔵 | - |
| 18.5 - 24.9 | Healthy Weight | Green 🟢 | - |
| 25.0 - 29.9 | Overweight | Orange 🟠 | - |
| ≥ 30.0 | Obese | Red 🔴 | "time to lock in and get the dream body!" |

<Warning>
BMI is a general indicator and doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition. Consult a healthcare professional for personalized health advice.
</Warning>

## Updating Your Profile

You can update your profile information at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile Tab">
    Navigate to the Profile tab from the bottom navigation.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Tap Edit">
    Tap the **Edit** button below your profile information.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Modify Fields">
    Update any fields you want to change. You can also switch between US and Metric units.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Tap **Save** to update your profile. A new BMI history entry is created automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## BMI History Tracking

Every time you update your profile, CalPal records:

- **Date/Time** of the update
- **Weight** at that time
- **Height** at that time
- **Calculated BMI**

This creates a historical record you can view on the Analytics screen.

<Tip>
Update your profile weekly to track trends over time. CalPal will remind you if it's been more than 7 days since your last update.
</Tip>

## Weekly Update Prompts

CalPal includes a smart reminder system:

- If you haven't updated your profile in **7 days**, you'll see a prompt
- The prompt asks: "Would you like to log your current weight and height?"
- You can choose **Yes** to update or **No** to dismiss
- The prompt won't appear again for another 7 days

<Note>
Weekly prompts help you maintain consistent tracking without being annoying. You're always in control of when you update.
</Note>

## Viewing BMI History

See your BMI trends over time on the **Analytics** tab:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Analytics">
    Tap the Analytics tab in the bottom navigation.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Open Weight Over Time">
    Expand the "Weight Over Time" collapsible section.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select Time Range">
    Choose from:
    - **1W** - Last 7 days
    - **1M** - Last 30 days
    - **1Y** - Last 365 days
    - **ALL** - Complete history
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="View Chart">
    An interactive line chart shows your weight trends. Tap data points to see exact values.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Interactive Chart Features

- **Tap data points** to see exact date, weight, and BMI
- **Color-coded line** matches your BMI category
- **Responsive scaling** adjusts to your data range
- **Date labels** show when each measurement was taken

<Tip>
The chart requires at least 2 data points. Keep updating your profile weekly to see meaningful trends.
</Tip>

## Privacy & Data Storage

<Info>
Your health data is **100% private** and stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and WatermelonDB. CalPal never sends your personal health information to external servers.
</Info>

### What's Stored Locally

- Age, height, weight, unit preference
- Complete BMI history with timestamps
- Last prompt date for weekly reminders

Notice at the bottom of the profile editor:

> "No data is stored or sent anywhere. Everything is stored locally."

## Clearing Your Data

If you want to start fresh:

<Warning>
Clearing your data is permanent and cannot be undone. This will delete your profile, all food logs, and BMI history.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile Tab">
    Navigate to the Profile screen.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Tap Clear All Data">
    Tap the "Clear All Data" option in the settings list.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm that you want to permanently delete all data.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="App Resets">
    CalPal will reset to initial state and prompt you to create a new profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Effective Health Tracking

<Tip>
**Weigh Yourself Consistently** - Weigh at the same time of day (preferably morning, before eating) for accurate comparisons.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Track Weekly, Not Daily** - Weight fluctuates daily due to water retention, meals, etc. Weekly tracking shows real trends.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Combine with Food Logs** - Use CalPal's food logging to see how your eating habits affect your weight over time.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Take Measurements Too** - BMI doesn't tell the whole story. Consider tracking waist circumference or body fat percentage separately.
</Tip>

## Understanding Your BMI

BMI is a useful screening tool, but has limitations:

**BMI is helpful for:**
- General population health screening
- Tracking trends over time
- Identifying potential health risks

**BMI doesn't account for:**
- Muscle mass (athletes may have high BMI but low body fat)
- Bone density
- Age and sex differences
- Body fat distribution

<Note>
Use BMI as one indicator among many. Combine it with other health metrics and consult healthcare professionals for personalized guidance.
</Note>
