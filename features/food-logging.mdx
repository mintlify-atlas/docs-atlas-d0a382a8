Manual food logging is the foundation of CalPal's tracking system. This feature allows you to enter complete nutritional details for any food item, giving you full control over your data.

## Opening the Food Log Modal

You can access manual food logging from the home screen by:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the Add Button">
    Tap the floating action button (FAB) with the + icon in the bottom-right corner of the home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'Add Food Manually'">
    From the menu that appears, choose "Add Food Manually" to open the food log form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Required Fields

The following fields are **required** to create a valid food log:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Food Name" icon="utensils">
    The name or description of the food item
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cost" icon="dollar-sign">
    How much you paid for the food in USD
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weight" icon="weight-scale">
    The weight of the food in grams
  </Card>
  <Card title="Calories" icon="fire">
    Total caloric content
  </Card>
  <Card title="Protein" icon="drumstick-bite">
    Protein content in grams
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
The "Add" button will remain disabled until all required fields are filled in.
</Note>

## Optional Fields

You can optionally add more detailed macronutrient information:

- **Carbohydrates** (g) - Total carb content
- **Fat** (g) - Total fat content

## Logging Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Food Name">
    Type the name of the food item in the first field. This is what will be displayed in your log history.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Cost">
    Enter the cost in USD. This helps track your food spending over time.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Weight">
    Input the weight in grams. This is used for portion tracking.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Calories">
    Enter the total calorie content. You can find this on nutrition labels or food databases.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter Protein">
    Input the protein content in grams. This is essential for tracking your daily protein intake.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Add Optional Macros">
    If desired, add carbohydrate and fat content for complete nutritional tracking.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap the "Add" button to save your food log. It will be timestamped and added to your daily log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing Food Logs

You can edit any existing food log from your log history:

1. Navigate to the food log card in your "Recent Days" section
2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋯) in the top-right corner of the card
3. Select **Edit** from the menu
4. Modify any fields you want to change
5. Tap **Save** to update the log

<Info>
When editing a food log, all the same validation rules apply - you must fill in all required fields.
</Info>

## Deleting Food Logs

To remove a food log:

1. Tap the three-dot menu (⋯) on the food log card
2. Select **Delete**
3. Confirm the deletion in the alert dialog

<Warning>
Deleting a food log is permanent and cannot be undone. Your summary statistics will be updated immediately.
</Warning>

## Tips for Accurate Logging

<Tip>
Use a food scale to weigh your portions for the most accurate tracking. This makes a significant difference in calorie and macro calculations.
</Tip>

<Tip>
Check nutrition labels carefully - serving sizes may differ from the entire package. Adjust your entries accordingly.
</Tip>

<Tip>
Log your food immediately after eating to avoid forgetting details or portion sizes.
</Tip>
