CalPal's barcode scanning feature allows you to instantly log packaged foods by scanning their barcodes. The app automatically retrieves nutritional information from the USDA FoodData Central database.

## How It Works

Barcode scanning integrates with the USDA FoodData Central API to provide accurate nutritional data for millions of branded and packaged food products.

<Info>
CalPal uses the USDA FoodData Central API, which includes comprehensive data for branded foods, restaurant items, and survey data from the Food and Nutrient Database for Dietary Studies (FNDDS).
</Info>

## Starting a Barcode Scan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Scanner">
    From the home screen, tap the floating + button and select **"Scan Barcode"** from the menu.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Grant Camera Permission">
    If this is your first time scanning, you'll be prompted to grant camera access. Tap **"Grant Permission"** to allow CalPal to use your camera.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Position the Barcode">
    Hold your device steady and position the barcode within the camera frame. The scanner supports multiple barcode formats.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Wait for Detection">
    The scanner will automatically detect and process the barcode. You'll see the camera view close once scanning is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Scanning

Once a barcode is scanned, CalPal performs the following steps:

1. **Searches USDA Database** - The barcode is sent to USDA FoodData Central to find matching products
2. **Retrieves Product Details** - If found, the app fetches the full nutritional profile including:
   - Product name and brand
   - Calories (kcal)
   - Protein (g)
   - Carbohydrates (g)
   - Fat (g)
3. **Prompts for Cost** - You'll be asked to enter how much you paid for the item
4. **Creates Log Entry** - The food log is automatically created with all nutritional data

<Note>
If the product isn't found in the USDA database, you'll be prompted to enter the nutritional information manually.
</Note>

## Search Instead of Scan

If you can't scan a barcode or prefer to search manually:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Using Search Bar">
    When the scanner modal opens, you'll see a search bar at the top. Type the product name to search the USDA database without scanning.
    
    The search returns up to 10 results from both branded products and FNDDS survey data.
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Selecting Results">
    Tap any search result to view its nutritional information. The app will fetch detailed data and prompt you for the cost before creating the log entry.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Camera Permission Required

Barcode scanning requires camera access to function:

<Warning>
Without camera permission, you won't be able to scan barcodes. You can still use the search feature within the scanner modal, or add food manually.
</Warning>

If you previously denied camera access:

1. Go to your device Settings
2. Find CalPal in the app list
3. Enable Camera permissions
4. Return to CalPal and try scanning again

## Scanning Tips

<Tip>
**Good Lighting** - Scan in well-lit areas for best results. Poor lighting can make barcodes difficult to read.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Hold Steady** - Keep your phone steady and at the right distance. Most barcodes scan best from 6-12 inches away.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Clean Barcodes** - Make sure the barcode isn't damaged, wrinkled, or covered. Flatten any curved surfaces if possible.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Try Different Angles** - If a barcode won't scan, try tilting your phone slightly or adjusting the distance.
</Tip>

## Data Source Details

Nutritional data is sourced from:

- **USDA FoodData Central** - Official government nutrition database
- **Branded Foods** - Direct from manufacturers and retailers
- **FNDDS Survey Data** - From national dietary surveys

All nutritional values are standardized to provide:
- Calories in kcal
- Macronutrients in grams (g)
- Consistent serving size information
