Welcome to CalPal! This guide will walk you through the essential steps to start tracking your food, nutrition, and health metrics.

<Note>
  This quickstart assumes you've already installed CalPal. If not, see the [Installation Guide](/installation) first.
</Note>

## Getting Started with CalPal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch CalPal and Grant Permissions">
    When you first open CalPal, you'll be prompted to grant camera permissions. This is required for barcode scanning.
    
    **On iOS:**
    - Tap **Allow** when prompted for camera access
    
    **On Android:**
    - Tap **While using the app** or **Only this time**
    
    <Warning>
      Without camera permissions, you won't be able to use the barcode scanner feature. You can still log foods manually or use AI text entry.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Profile">
    CalPal will prompt you to create your user profile. This information helps calculate your BMI and track your health over time.
    
    **Required Information:**
    - **Age** - Your current age in years
    - **Height** - In feet/inches (US) or centimeters (Metric)
    - **Weight** - In pounds (US) or kilograms (Metric)
    - **Unit System** - Choose between US Standard or Metric
    
    Your BMI will be calculated automatically and displayed with color coding:
    - 🔵 **Blue**: Underweight (BMI < 18.5)
    - 🟢 **Green**: Normal (BMI 18.5-24.9)
    - 🟠 **Orange**: Overweight (BMI 25-29.9)
    - 🔴 **Red**: Obese (BMI ≥ 30)
    
    <Info>
      All profile data is stored **locally on your device**. CalPal never sends your personal information to any server.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log Your First Food">
    Now you're ready to log your first food! CalPal offers three ways to add foods:
    
    ### Method 1: Barcode Scanning (Recommended)
    
    1. Tap the **+** button in the bottom-right corner
    2. Select **Scan Barcode**
    3. Point your camera at the product's barcode
    4. CalPal will automatically fetch nutritional information from the USDA database
    5. Enter the cost when prompted
    6. Tap **Add** to save the log
    
    **Supported Barcode Types:**
    - UPC-A, UPC-E
    - EAN-8, EAN-13
    - Code 39, Code 128
    - QR codes
    
    ### Method 2: AI Natural Language Entry
    
    1. Tap the **+** button
    2. Select **Tell the AI**
    3. Describe what you ate in plain English
    
    **Example inputs:**
    ```
    "a chicken burrito bowl with rice and beans, plus a coke"
    "large pepperoni pizza from dominos, 3 slices"
    "grilled salmon with broccoli and quinoa"
    "starbucks grande caramel latte with oat milk"
    ```
    
    The AI will automatically extract:
    - Food name
    - Estimated cost
    - Calories
    - Protein, carbs, and fat content
    
    <Tip>
      Be specific! Mention portion sizes, cooking methods, and brands for more accurate nutritional estimates.
    </Tip>
    
    ### Method 3: Manual Entry
    
    1. Tap the **+** button
    2. Select **Add Food Manually**
    3. Fill in the food details:
       - **Name** (required)
       - **Cost** (required)
       - **Weight/Serving Size** (optional)
       - **Calories** (optional)
       - **Protein** (optional)
       - **Carbs** (optional)
       - **Fat** (optional)
    4. Tap **Save**
    
    <Info>
      You can edit or delete any food log by tapping the ⋯ menu on the food card.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="View Your Dashboard">
    After logging your first food, you'll see it appear on your **Home** screen dashboard.
    
    The dashboard shows:
    - **Summary stats** for your selected time period (1D, 1W, 1M, All)
    - **Total spending** on food
    - **Estimated weight change** based on calories
    - **Total calories, fat, carbs, and protein**
    - **Recent days** with quick date navigation
    - **Food logs** grouped by day
    
    Toggle between time frames to see different views:
    - **1D** - Last 24 hours
    - **1W** - Last 7 days
    - **1M** - Last 30 days
    - **All** - All-time data
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore Analytics">
    Navigate to the **Analytics** tab (third icon in the bottom navigation) to see:
    
    - **Weight over time** - Interactive chart showing your weight/BMI history
    - **Tips for healthy eating** - Personalized recommendations
    - **Time period filters** - View data for 1 week, 1 month, 1 year, or all time
    
    <Tip>
      Update your weight weekly through the Profile screen to see accurate weight trends over time!
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Success

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Log consistently for best results">
    Try to log every food item you consume, even small snacks. Consistency is key to understanding your eating patterns and making informed decisions about your nutrition.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Update your weight weekly">
    CalPal prompts you to update your weight and height every 7 days. This helps maintain accurate BMI trends and weight change calculations.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Use barcode scanning when possible">
    Barcode scanning provides the most accurate nutritional data since it pulls from the USDA FoodData Central database. Reserve manual entry for homemade foods or items without barcodes.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Review your analytics regularly">
    Check the Analytics tab weekly to spot trends in your spending, calorie intake, and macro distribution. Use these insights to adjust your eating habits.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Explore restaurant menus before ordering">
    Use the Restaurant Menus feature to compare nutritional options before placing an order. Filter by calories and protein to find meals that fit your goals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
